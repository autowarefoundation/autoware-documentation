## Capabilities

Autoware handles the full autonomous driving stack, from sensing to control.

<div class="aw-features" markdown>

- :fa-cl-s fa-robot: **AI & learning**

  Support for **End-to-End (E2E) driving models**, ML-based perception, and data-driven trajectory prediction.

- :fa-cl-s fa-eye: **Perception**

  **Multi-Sensor Fusion** (Lidar, Camera, Radar), Traffic Light Recognition, and dynamic object tracking.

- :fa-cl-s fa-location-dot: **Localization**

  High-precision **NDT matching** combined with GNSS/IMU odometry for robust positioning in mapped environments.

- :fa-cl-s fa-route: **Planning**

  **Behavior Planning** for intersections and lane changes, plus real-time **Dynamic Obstacle Avoidance**.

- :fa-cl-s fa-car: **Control**

  Precise **Trajectory Following** and standardized vehicle interfaces for drive-by-wire platforms.

- :fa-cl-s fa-gamepad: **Simulation**

  **Digital Twin Simulation** (AWSIM), Rosbag Replay, and Scenario Simulation for validation.

</div>
